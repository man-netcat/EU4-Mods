# Formables

Always **ask the user for exact requirements** before building one: land (areas or provinces), starters, percent,
capital, rank, CK3 title. Never assume them.

## Tables (`tools/tags.db`)

- `formables` columns: `tag, name, adj, ck3_title, rank, color_r/g/b, percent, capital, own_capital, culture, dynasty,
  culture_group, home_capital, rank_required, exclusive`.
- Requirement land: `formable_areas(formable, area)` and `formable_provinces(formable, pid)`.
- Renames: `formable_renames(formable, culture, tag, name, province)` (former tag/culture/province -> localisation key
  shown through `override_country_name`, most specific first).
- Starters point at a formable through `tags.forms` and `tags.decision` (e.g. `ASU` -> `LEO`, `kar_form_leon`); all
  starters of one formable share one decision key and one generated file `decisions/Form<TAG>.txt`
  (`histgen.formation_decision`; never edit it).
- `tag_suppress(tag, key)` suppresses a base-game formation decision for that tag; the base game's own decision files are
  neutralised by same-filename dummies (`VANILLA_FORMATION_FILES` in `steps/vanilla_scripts.py`).

## Semantics

- `percent` (NULL = all) is the share of requirement land the former must own, rounded up; `own_capital` also requires
  holding the formable's `capital` and moves the capital there; `home_capital` requires the firing tag's capital inside the
  requirement land (excludes `own_capital`); `rank_required` (3 = kingdom, -1 = none); `exclusive` makes starters mutual
  rivals; gating is by listed starters, a primary `culture`, `culture_group`s, or a `dynasty`.
- Requirement provinces become scripted triggers in `common/scripted_triggers/KarolingianFormations.txt`
  (`steps/formations.py`); validate checks the enacted percent, the land and the capital line.
- Custom formations without a row: HLR (`kar_form_hre`, generated by `steps/empire.py`), FIN, ROM (`FormROM.txt`),
  HUN (Magyar settlement, `steps/custom.py`).
- A tag has one `forms`, so Asturias that takes Leon cannot also take Spain. A formable's code must not collide with a
  tag of ours; a base-game tag that is a formable keeps that code (so a duchy named like it keeps another code: LTU/LIT,
  BHM/BOH, CST/CAS).
- Formables hold no land at the start; selfcheck needs them to have areas or provinces (HLR excepted). Placeholder flags
  cover formables too (`placeholder_flags.py`).

After a change: `python3 tools/build.py && python3 tools/validate.py`. A starter whose ruler's culture misses the
formable's culture gate fails selfcheck: drop the starter link or keep the land culture, do not add an exception.
