# The Kingdom of Algarve was a separate crown from 1383, when Joao I inherited
# it alongside Portugal, and stayed a distinct kingdom in personal union with
# the Portuguese crown through the whole Aviz line. Only Maria I united the two
# titles for good in 1779, taking "King of Portugal and the Algarves".
#
# Modelled as a real personal union from 1444: the kingdom holds its own
# province, 230 Algarve, and sits in union with Portugal as the junior crown
# to the Portuguese one, with Afonso V on both thrones. The union is declared
# in history/diplomacy, not by a decision - the kingdom is never "restored",
# because it never goes away. Portugal deliberately keeps no core here: the two
# crowns are tied by the union alone, and a Portuguese core would let Lisbon
# simply annex the kingdom at leisure.
#
# Note the Algarve is a single province in EU4 (230, capital Faro). Vanilla's
# history/provinces/2933 - Faro.txt is misnamed - its contents are Barcelos, a
# colonial holding in the Caribbean - so it must not be given a core or an
# owner here.
