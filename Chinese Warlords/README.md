# Chinese Warlords Mod

A comprehensive mod for Europa Universalis IV that adds a set of Chinese warlord/releasable formable nations (including the Taiping Heavenly Kingdom) with unique mechanics, events, and historical flavor.

## Overview

The Taiping Heavenly Kingdom was a theocratic state in southern China that existed from 1851 to 1864 during the Taiping Rebellion. Led by Hong Xiuquan, who claimed to be the younger brother of Jesus Christ, it was one of the deadliest conflicts in history and represented a unique blend of Christian theology and Chinese revolutionary ideals.

## Features

### Formable Nation
- **Decision**: Form the Taiping Heavenly Kingdom with specific requirements
- **Requirements**:
  - Must be a theocracy
  - Control key historical provinces (Nanjing, Guiping, Guangzhou, Changsha, Wuchang)
  - Have Christian or Harmonized Christianity religion
  - Total development of 200+
  - Recent military conflicts or rebellions
  
### National Ideas
The Taiping get 8 unique national ideas reflecting their historical characteristics:
1. **The Heavenly Kingdom** - Legitimacy/Devotion and Land Morale
2. **God's Chinese Son** - Religious tolerance and Church Power
3. **Equal Land Distribution** - Production efficiency and Development cost
4. **The Heavenly Army** - Infantry power and Force limit
5. **Radical Equality** - Unrest reduction and Stability cost
6. **Missionary Zeal** - Extra missionary and missionary strength
7. **Revolutionary Ideals** - Reduced liberty desire
8. **Building the New Society** - Advisor cost and Reform progress

### Unique Mission Tree
A complete mission tree with three branches:
- **Military Branch**: Secure Tianjing, dominate southern China, control the Yangzi, launch northern expeditions
- **Religious Branch**: Spread Christianity, destroy idols, convert China
- **Economic Branch**: Land reform, build equal society, create prosperity

### Gameplay

This mod adds the Taiping Heavenly Kingdom as a formable nation and releasable tag. It uses base game events and mechanics for spawning and gameplay, similar to how other Chinese warlord states function.

### Country Modifiers
Multiple unique modifiers including:
- Taiping Revolution
- Land Reform
- Heavenly Mandate
- Missionary Fervor
- Military Reforms
- And more...

## Historical Accuracy

This mod incorporates many historical elements of the Taiping Heavenly Kingdom:
- **Religion**: Christian-influenced theocracy with unique beliefs
- **Social Reforms**: Gender equality, land redistribution, communal economics
- **Military**: The Heavenly Army and major campaigns like the Northern Expedition
- **Leadership**: Hong Xiuquan as the Heavenly King and other historical princes
- **Geography**: Historical provinces and capital at Tianjing (Nanjing)
- **Ideology**: Radical equality, religious zeal, revolutionary ideals

## Installation

1. Download the mod
2. Place the "Chinese Warlords" folder in your EU4 mod directory:
   - Windows: `Documents/Paradox Interactive/Europa Universalis IV/mod/`
   - Linux: `~/.local/share/Paradox Interactive/Europa Universalis IV/mod/`
   - Mac: `~/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Europa Universalis IV/mod/`
3. Copy "Chinese Warlords.mod" to the same directory
4. Enable the mod in the EU4 launcher
5. (Optional) Add a custom flag for TAI in `gfx/flags/`

## Gameplay Tips

### Forming Taiping
- Start as China/Qing and convert to Christianity or harmonize with it
- Reform government to theocracy
- Conquer the required southern provinces
- Ensure you have recent wars or rebellions to represent the revolutionary context
- Use the decision when all requirements are met

### As Taiping
- Focus on religious conversion to maximize bonuses
- Use land reform events to boost economy
- Expand through southern China first before northern campaigns
- Balance religious zeal with practical governance
- Watch for internal power struggles

### For China/Qing Players
- The Taiping Rebellion event can fire if conditions are met
- You can either suppress it militarily or release them as tributary
- Prepare for a major civil war if you choose to fight
- Keep southern provinces stable to reduce rebellion risk
