# Form Slovenia

A Europa Universalis IV mod that adds Slovenia as a formable nation.

## Features

- **New Formable Nation**: Slovenia (SLV) can be formed by nations with Slovene primary culture
- **Historical Provinces**: Requires control of the three core Slovene provinces:
  - Krain (129) - Ljubljana
  - Görz (1769)
  - Cilli (4751)
- **Unique National Ideas**: Slovenia comes with its own national idea set focused on:
  - Alpine defenses and mountain warfare
  - Trade advantages as a crossroads of Europe
  - Cultural preservation
  - Administrative efficiency
- **Requirements**:
  - Primary culture must be Slovene
  - Must own and core all three Slovene provinces
  - Must be at peace
  - Must have Administrative Technology 10
  - Cannot be the Holy Roman Emperor or Papal States
  - Must be independent or a tributary

## Effects

When forming Slovenia:
- Changes tag to SLV (Slovenia)
- Capital moves to Ljubljana (Krain)
- Becomes a Kingdom (if not already)
- Gains 25 prestige
- Gains the "Centralization Modifier" for 20 years
- Gains permanent claims on the Carinthia area
- Swaps to Slovenian national ideas (if not using custom ideas)
- Removes from HRE if not an elector

## Installation

1. Place the mod files in your Europa Universalis IV mod folder
2. Enable the mod in the EU4 launcher
3. Start a new game or load an existing save

## Compatible With

- EU4 version 1.37.* and later
- Should be compatible with most other mods

## Historical Context

The Slovene people historically inhabited the regions of Carniola, Carinthia, and Gorizia, mostly under Habsburg rule. While Slovenia as a unified nation-state didn't exist during the EU4 timeframe, this mod allows players to unite the Slovene-speaking territories and create an independent Slovenian nation.
