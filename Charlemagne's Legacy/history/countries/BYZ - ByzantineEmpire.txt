# 867 court installed as the 1444 start state, the same thing every other
# transferred tag does in this mod (BUL's Boris Balgarsko, CRT's Abu Hafs Umar,
# ARB's Mu'tazz, EGY's Ahmad Tulunid, ADU's Nayih). Basileios is taken from
# CK3 character 1700, who at 867.1.1 holds both e_byzantium and c_byzantion:
# he is already the emperor in the source we draw from, so he needs no
# promotion or reachability argument, only translation.
#
# Name and spelling are his as CK3 writes them: "Basileios", not the anglicised
# "Basil". The 867 block above uses the same spelling for consistency, and its
# dynasty string is corrected from "Macedonian" to "Makedon" to match CK3
# dynasty 644, whose localisation key dynn_Makedon is "Makedon".
#
# Stats read off his CK3 traits: education_martial_2, strong, brave, ambitious,
# murderer, deceitful, hunter. That is a soldier-emperor - competent in the
# field, ruthless, and no administrator - so military leads and diplomacy
# trails, which is also how Basil I actually came to the throne. His two
# marriages in CK3 (145131 and 1761) and the fact that he was a general before
# he was a basileus are both read into the adm/mil split rather than given a
# flat 2/2/2.
#
# The heir is Leon (CK3 character 1702, born_in_the_purple,
# education_learning_3), the eldest of his sons. Leon is a study, not a soldier,
# which is why the adm/dip lead and mil sits lowest - the deliberate opposite of
# his father, and the reason a 867 court can lose a war to the Ottomans later.
